🧫 Microscopic Life Lab
Cells merge when they touch. The brain preserves an organism's basic shape and helps it move. The translucent green body connects touching cells. Use 🔺 Head and 🔻 Bottom to assign brain roles, then 🧠 Give Brain. Use 🪨 Terrain as solid dirt obstacles. Use 🗑️ Delete Terrain to remove them. Use 📏 Scale with the slider above a cell to resize it, ➗ Divide to split one cell into two smaller cells, 🎨 Color to choose a preset color, 🚫 Unconnectable to stop a cell from forming new connections while keeping its existing links, and 🔗 Connectable to restore a cell so it can form new connections again, and ⚓ Anchor/Unanchor to pin cells. Use 🦴 Bone to make rigid attached structures such as jaws, legs, mouths, or antennae; bones that join two cells keep a fixed length instead of acting like springs. Connector lines show the actual cell-to-cell links.
